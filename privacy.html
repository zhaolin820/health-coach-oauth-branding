Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Health Coach Google Health is a personal OAuth client for a single Google account. Health data accessed through the Google Health API stays between that Google account and the local machine running the open-source google-health-mcp connector. No third-party hosted service operated by this app stores your health data.

Data we access

With your consent, the local connector may read and write Google Health data you authorize, such as activity, sleep, heart metrics, nutrition, hydration, and weight.

Storage

OAuth tokens and client credentials are stored locally on the machine that runs the connector (for example under ~/.config/google-health-mcp/). They are not uploaded to a Health Coach cloud service by this branding site.

Sharing

This personal app does not sell or share your health data. Google’s handling of Health data is governed by Google’s own policies.
